How to use?
Press enter on the .exe file. Stella bot will connect to the game.
Then, you will see chat, misc and kills messages
Press f1 to open bot commands dialog.
Press f2 to hide or unhide the bot, note! Not hide the bot with command /hideme, because then stella check will not work, and when stella will be connected, other people will see stella is official bot bla bla!
Press f3 to enable/disable debug mode. When this mode enabled, you will see all the packets: stepps, jumps, etc.
Press f4 to coppy buffer item.
Press space to enable or disable bot speach.
Press enter to send chat message, or shift enter to send a packet to the game.
Press comma and dot to move forward or back in your message history. Note, also saving packets.
Press left/right brackets to move thrue the buffers.
Press escape to exit.

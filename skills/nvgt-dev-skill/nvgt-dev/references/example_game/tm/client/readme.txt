now when you have been fimiliarised with the controls, let's jump in to the key topics which you need to know for successfully playing the game! ready? here we go!
the coordinates
the coordinates are the main guiding tools which allow you to know your location. every location or area is located on different coordinates. for example, the story mode is located in the safe zone, at 10, 10, 0. these coordinates can be checked by pressing C, and can be changed by moving around. for example, if you're standing at 0, 0, 0, and walk five steps forward by holding or pressing the up aero, your coordinates will be 0, 5, 0. coordinates might be a difficult thing to understand if you are a new player, however with enough practice and some time it will become second nature!
tracking
since it is a fighting game first and formost, it is necessary to know how to kill. to kill someone you need to know where they are. that's where tracking comes in. when you track a player you can constently check where a player is going, how farther away is he from you, and if he is in front or behind. to track a player, bring up the players menu by pressing t or shift plus t, choose the player you want to track, and hit enter. it will confirm that it is tracking that particular player. after that, to know where that player is or how far he is, just keep pressing w to know his precise location
inventory
your inventory is where all your items are kept. your weapons, your vehicles, whatever you have, is kept in your invitory. to bring up your invitory press I. it will provide a list of all the items contained in the invitory. to use an item, press enter on it. you will here a zip sound. after that, press enter again, and the item will be used. keep in mind that some items cannot be used on specific maps. for example, you cannot use vehicles or weapons in the safe zone
wand
the wand is a very useful and deadly object in the game. it is an item gained through the completion of the story mode, filled with magical properties. you can cast all sorts of spells with the wand. to heal your health, to kill other players, or to even send them to hell. to use the wand, you need to have both the wand and an item called aura in your inventory. the aura is the enerjy which the wand needs to cast spells. each spell requires a certain amount of aura. if you do not have that amount you cannot cast the spell. to activate the wand menu, press the key x. a list of all the spells and what they do will be provided, including how much aura they require to be casted. a list of every spell and what it does will not be provided here, as it already exists in the game.
weapons.
there are two type of weapons in the game. melee weapons and long ranged weapons. melee weapons are of course, swords, knives, hammers, sabres, etc. long ranged weapons include machineguns, lasers, snipers, and all other sorts of guns. using a weapon is simple. firstly, you open your invitory, select the weapon of your choice, and hit enter on it. once the invitory closes, hit enter again, and your weapon will be drawn. now, press the key z to check how many ammo it has. if it does not requires ammo or is already reloaded that's fantastic, however if it declares the ammo count 0, press the key r to reload the weapon. please note that if you do not have the ammo for the weapon in your inventory you'll not be able to reload it, unless you perchase some ammo of course. now, once you've reloaded it, you're ready to go on a killing spree. track a player, and start hammering away at him! to fire the weapon press the control key
weapons and tracking.
of course, you can't just fire your weapon in a random direction and expect to massacre everyone now can you? your aim needs to be perfect, or almost perfect. when you fire your weapon, it will hit a player only in two conditions. the first is that the targeted player needs to be in the range of  the weapon. every weapon has a different range, so you have to be careful with that. as long as the player you're trying to hit is out of range you will not be able to hit him. same goes for creatures and animals.
the second thing you need to keep in mind is that to successfully hit a player, you need to keep him in certain directions. remember how we discussed earlier that you can use q and e to change your directions? the same thing applies here. keep changing your direction until you have the player in the right one. as you change directions, the status of the player you're tracking with w will also change. for example, when you first started tracking him he might have been straight behind. but after you change directions a cupple of times? he could be in front and a little ways off to the left, straight off to the right, or straight in front. to hit a player with a weapon, you need to keep him straight in front, or in front and very slightly to the left or right.
story mode
the story mode is one of the prime features of the massacre 2.0. consisting of five quests, it contains difficult challenges, interesting maps, and a huge amount of deadly fighting! on the completion of each quest you get a reward, and when you complete the story mode you are gifted with the wand.
the look and trust system
the look and trust system is designed so players can watch other players doing something. for example, imagine you don't feel like playing much. you could sit comfortably eating your favorite snacks, all the while you could watch other players attempting to cross different challenges, trying their luck at story mode, or even fighting for their life against another player or a deadly creature. to watch someone, the player needs to trust you first. the commands to use this system are as follows.
/trust playername. typing this makes you trust the player of your choice. for example, /trust pepsy. now, I know no self-respecting person would create a username like this, but for example I think it will be alright..plus who knows, maybe someone might be named that..but back to the topic, if you type / trust pepsy, you will be able to trust pepsy. however if pepsy is not online the command will do absolutely nothing.
/look playername. typing this will enable you to watch the player of your choice. for example, typing /look pepsy wil cause you to follow pepsy around. in this state you will not be able to move, neither able to attack anyone. same goes with others. when you are looking at someone noone can attack you.
/lookstop. this will cause you to stop looking at people, taking you back to where you were before.
/trustlist. brings up a list of all the players whom you trust.
/untrust playername. this will remove the player's name from your trust list
skateboard
I think everyone will be fimiliar with what a skateboard is and what its used for, so instead of boring you to death, I'll jump to the commands straight away. enter to clime on the skateboard, backspace to get off, up aero to go forward, and that's it. note, avoid crashing in to walls, because if you do your skateboard will crash...yeah, that happens when you crash in to walls. things get broken.
vehicles
the game has a lot of vehicles, in which you can travel around. vehicles can also be used to fight.
to use a vehicle, bring up your invitory, hit enter on the vehicle you wish to use, then hit enter again to activate it. after that, press e to start it, left and right aeros to change directions, up aero to go forward, and down aero to stop the vehicle. when you want to get out of your vehicle, press down aero until your vehicle is not moving, then press e to turn it off, finally pressing escape to get out. 
vehicles and weapons.
the vehicles can also be used to fight, like mentioned above. some of the vehicles have their own weapons. press the space key to fire using the vehicle's weapon. your normal weapons can also be used in a vehicle. the process is the same as using them normally. draw them, reload, track a player and start hammering away!
The Camera
The camera allows you to see what's around you in the game. It can be useful for looking for edges of platforms, above or below you, and locating a multitude of other things. Here's how it works.
To use your camera, you must be holding the G Key.
While the G key is down, the following keys  can be used:
Up, down, left and right arrows: Move your camera. It will play sounds at the camera's location on what it finds, e.g. walls, air, tiles, hazard, etc.
Page Up / Down: Moves your camera up or down.
V: Repeats the sound at the camera's current location.
F: Speaks what is at the camera's current tile coordinates.
favorite items
the favorite items are a bunch of items which you can assign specific numbers to. for example, let's say you have a favorite gun. instead of going through your invitory whenever you want to use it, you can set it as a favorite item. to set a favorite item, open your invitory and hit enter on an item, then use shift plus a number like 1 to 0 to assign the item to. after the favorite item has been assigned, you can use the item without opening your invitory
auctioning
the game has a complete auction system, where players can auction their items at different prices. often the items which are difficult to gain in the game can be obtained through auctioning. to auction an item, type /auction amount item name minimam bid. for example, if you want to auction 15 suicide bombs, with the minimam bid set to 100000, type /auction 15 suicide_bomb 100000

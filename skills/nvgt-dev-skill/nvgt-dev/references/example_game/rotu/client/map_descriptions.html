Revenge of the Undead main map descriptions
Last updated on Thursday November 26th

Main1

Main 1 is the default starting map, and it is the easiest to grasp. It is 500 x 500 y and is easily layed out for new players and experts alike.
The map is set in a city. The city is about 465 tiles long on the y axis. The city's border is at 466, and the outer portion is the city outskirts, where a tall, steep hill takes place. In the city itself there are about 12 buildings of interest, with more coming.
The hill has nothing much on it, however you will find two caves, one at the bottom and one near the bottom of the hill. Atop the hill is Emily Sparrow's house, and at the way northeastern corner of the hilltop is the entrance to the PVP (player versus player) map.
The safe zone is roughly in the center of the main map on the north side, not too far from the city's northern border, north of Denver street. It is surrounded by barricades, which guard the safe zone from undead. The entrance to the safe zone is located on one of the safe zone's outer walls on the front side, which is at 222, 427, 0.
The safe zone also has a roof, which can be located by climbing either the west or east ladder. Directly when you step into the safe zone by pressing Shift Enter on its door, to your west or east, is a ladder, which leads up to the rooftop.
Behind the safe zone is a small walk-in workshop for car repairs. For more information about cars refer to section 3.5 of the readme. The outpost workshop has a little staircase in one of its corners that leads up to the outpost roof. On top of that roof is yet another staircase, leading right back up to the safe zone's rooftop.
In the southwest corner of the city, you will find the outpost, the temple, and an abandoned tent.
The main road of the city runs directly down the middle of the map, where you can find two intersections. One is Olivia avenue, and the other is Denver street.
Olivia avenue has two opposite sidewalks, one on the north and one on the south. The north sidewalk is a very long row of abandoned houses, with the main road separating the two groups. The south sidewalk has other buildings of interest, such as the cafeteria to the west and the super market to the east. Between both buildings is the Nuggeteer fast food.
Also on the mid western side of the map, on Olivia avenue, you will find the entrance to main3, which is a desert. No experience is required to enter this map.
Opposite main on the same road, to the far east, is the entrance to main4, a warehouse. As you can already guess, it is full of undead, and the interior is rather small. You need 10000 experience to access this map.
IN the northwestern corner of the city outskirts, just beyond the city wall, you will find the entrance to main2, a harder map with more obstacles. You must be a Specialist (3000 experience) or higher to enter this map. You will also find an abandoned truck with a gravestone nearby, and you will see why in main2.

Main2

The main2 map is much more difficult, with much more obstacles to dodge around. The spaces are also pretty tight, making it harder to fight and easier to be swarmed.
There is not much to explore here, as it is an overgrown forest and things are so crowded anyways. However, the river (which is the safe zone) is somewhere in the middle of the map, surrounded by some branches and stone.
To the far north, the entrance to main5 is accessible. It's a graveyard, which is where the abandoned truck was headed.
There is one area you have to watch out for though. At around 105 or so, there is a small overgrowth that has formed some sort of path. You have to climb onto it and then walk aways, all the way to 150. Once you reach 150, and jump off, you must time your jump correctly to get back onto the growth.

main3

Main3 is a very large desert with really nothing here, except the safe zone and a ruined temple in the mid northern area. The rock formation which is the safe zone, is located somewhere in the middle of the desert. However, do note that just because there are no obstacles and the map is empty, it is definitely full of undead, so watch out, and stay alert!
Main1 is accessible in the mid east area, which leads back into the city.
The desert is also a PVP map, so players are able to fight in this area. Another thing to be alert of.

main4

Main4 is a very small warehouse. And its brimming with undead, that are willing to swarm you!
The warehouse is divided into four sections, labeled A through D. Sections A and B are mostly areas full of shelves, barrels, and other important warehouse items. Section C is completely empty, its perhaps the easiest place to fight undead in terms of the map. Section D is full of crate stacks, and quality control. The northeastern section of the warehouse, considered Section E, but not necessarily called that, is the loading dock, where crates are brought into and out of the warehouse.
Along the southern and northern edges of the map are paths that allow you to move between the various sections. There are little concrete markings to let you know when you are near a section. Also do not forget to use the M key, to find the map beacons.
Main1 is back through the loading dock, the northeast section of the warehouse.

main5

Main5 is a graveyard, full of several headstones of long dead people who have died, either due to what Kyle did, or for other reasons. This map is pretty small, but it is also crawling with undead, so watch your step!

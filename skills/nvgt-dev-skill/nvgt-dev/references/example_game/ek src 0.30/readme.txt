Let's get into the game quickly. The game is a 3D war game where you can buy various weapons and fight against creatures or other players. You can challenge other teams with your team, earn money and have more powerful weapons.
The game features a keyboard layout found in many games with just a few differences.
Let's learn about the main menu!
The main menu of the game is a classic main menu.
You can use the Page Up and Page Down keys to adjust the volume of the main menu music. You can navigate through the options using the Up and Down Arrow keys and activate them with the Enter key.
"You have not accepted the Era of Knights rules. Press Enter to accept."
This button allows you to accept the game rules: When you press Yes in the dialog that opens, some buttons become visible.
Login Options
This button opens a menu with options to log in to the game, access the game, and create an account.
Options
This menu allows you to change game settings and customize options to your preference.
Server Menu
Here you can see which server you are connected to, whether the server is safe or not, and view public servers. You can switch between them.
Check for updates
This small button checks for game updates and allows you to download them if available. You can download sound updates and other updates from the main menu without leaving the game.
Test Speakers
A simple option to test your headphones. Click to test and there is always a button waiting for you.
exit
If you are bored of the game, it is too early to quit, but even the name of the button shows what it does.
Create a new account
What you need to do here is quite simple. Navigate using the tab key:
• Type the account name you want to be displayed in the game; spaces are not allowed. Find this field and enter the name you will use in the game; This is not your nickname.
You can go to the next step with the Enter or Next button.
Password Screen
Create a strong password that you can remember and do not share it with anyone. Be careful of potential scammers in the game. The password is your responsibility and you should not share your account with untrusted people. We are not responsible if someone misuses your items or account and it does not affect the staff. Your password must be at least 8 characters long and must not contain spaces.
If you have entered your password, you can continue by pressing the Next button.
Email Screen

After entering your email, press the Finish button!
Gender Section
Please choose the option that suits you. If you make the wrong choice, your rights may be restricted and you may not be able to use them again.
If everything is filled in correctly, find the Login button in the dialog that appears; you are ready!

First, let's learn how to control our character and everything about it!
Let's find out quickly:
• R: Sitting / Standing
• S:Indicates your thirst; If it reaches 1000, you will likely die or lose your Immortality Card.
• U: Indicates your hunger; similar to thirst; If it reaches 1000, you die or lose your Immortality Card.
•I; Allows you to open your inventory.
• E and Q: Turn 45 degrees.
• K: Allows you to reload your weapon if you have ammo.
• L: Shows your level and your remaining XP to the next level.
• semicolon: Opens the social menu where you can select and use various social actions, such as laughing or clapping.
• Enter: Allows you to use an item in your inventory.
• Shift + Enter: Starts an auction where you can sell items to other players for dollars.
• Tab: Shows descriptions of items, if any.
• Space:Allows you to pass an item you hover over to nearby players.
• Back button: Discards an item from your bag.
• Escape: Closes inventory.
C: Shows your X, Y, Z coordinates at your current location.
Shift + C: Copies everything in the buffer.
Slash: Sends a message in the public chat.
Backslash: Sends a message on the map.
Y: Allows you to enter and send commands.
Shift + Y: Sends a message to administrators. Use this when necessary.
N: Shows your current location.
M: Shows nearby items.
P: Shows nearby players.
O: Opens a menu to manage your settings.
T: If you are in a shelter, it opens the shelter menu.
X: Allows you to see places around you. If something is visible, select it and press Enter to follow it.
J: Provides information about the location you are following.
F: Shows your direction.
Space: Allows you to jump; jumping increases your z coordinate.
home/end: ​​used to adjust the volume of game sounds.

this sets the game music: if you use one it will set the game sound
F1: Shows the number of people on the server.
F2: Shows the server's uptime.
F3: Shows Ping.
F4 shows the game message set by administrators, it is possible to read it line by line
F5: Opens the follow menu where you can get information about the people you can follow.
F6: Opens the list of players in the game.
Player Menu:
• F1, F2, F3 and F4: provide small details about the player.
• M: Mutes a contact.
• U: Unmutes a contact.
• Ctrl + C: Copy a person's name.
• Enter: Sends a PM to the person.
• V: quickly responds to the last PM when the player menu is closed.
Left and Right Brackets: Switch between buffers.
comma and period: Scroll through messages in the buffer. The comma is the previous message and the period is the next message. Use the Shift key to jump to the beginning and end of the buffer.
F8: turns conversation on and off.
F9: Turns automatic walking on and off.
Z: Shows how many bullets you have left in your gun, how many you can load, and how many bullets you have in your bag if you have a gun.
A: Shows nearby creatures.
H: Indicates your health.
1 to 0: Assign favorite items to these keys for easy access. To add an item to your favorites, hold down the Shift key and click the number key you want to assign the item to. You have successfully added your favorite item!
